# College Football Live Scores for Pebble

Stuck at work on a Saturday? Keep your team's score on your wrist. College Football Live Scores updates every minute straight from ESPN's public scoreboard API, so a glance at your watch is all you need.

## Features

**Every FBS team** - all 138 FBS schools, from Air Force to Wyoming, sorted alphabetically with their conference.

**Rankings** - AP Top 25 rank shown next to each team's record ("#5 7-1") pre-game and after the final, and on every ticker entry.

**Never miss the big game** - when your team isn't playing (and isn't kicking off within 2 hours), the watchface automatically switches to the highest-ranked Top 25 game that's live right now. A "T25" tag folds into the status line so it's never mistaken for your own team's game, and it switches back on its own once your team's game gets close. Toggle: **Auto-Show Top 25 Games**.

**School colors** - team abbreviations and end zones drawn in each school's real color (Pebble Time 2). Schools whose primary color is black use their alternate color so they stay readable.

**Live game** - score, quarter & clock, down & distance, and the last play - shortened to just the name that matters and the yardage ("Smith 12 yds"). "Halftime" at the half, and college overtime periods show as "OT", "2OT", "3OT"...

**Team stats** - each team's passing and rushing yards ("P:220 R:95") sit right under the score during a live game. Turnovers ("INT:2 FUMB:1") get their own line below that (Pebble Time 2).

**Field position bar** - end zones, yard lines, a football marker with a `>`/`<` arrow showing which way the offense is driving, and a red outline whenever a team is in the red zone.

**Pre-game** - kickoff time, TV network, rank + record.

**Final** - final score and your next scheduled game (including conference championships and bowls).

**Bye week** - "Bye Week" with your next opponent and kickoff day.

**Top 25 ticker** - right under the time and date, cycling through the week's ranked matchups (live games first). Pace: **Ticker Speed** (5 / 10 / 30 / 60 seconds).

**Notifications** - 1 short buzz for a field goal or safety, 3 short buzzes for a touchdown; only ever for your own team. Toggle: **Vibrate on Score**.

**Always visible** - time, date, and an optional battery bar. Toggle: **Battery Bar**.

## Settings

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Team | All 138 FBS teams |
| Auto-Show Top 25 Games | On / Off |
| Ticker Speed | 5s / 10s / 30s / 60s |
| Vibrate on Score | On / Off |
| Battery Bar | On / Off |

## Data

ESPN's public college-football scoreboard/summary/schedule API - free, no API key. Refreshes every minute.
